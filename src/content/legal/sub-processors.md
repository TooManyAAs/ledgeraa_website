## 5. Changes to Sub-processors

5.1 In accordance with clause 4 of the DPA, Ledgeraa shall update this page, and notify you by email to the address registered to your Account or by notice within the Service, not less than 30 days before:

(a) engaging a new Sub-processor;

(b) replacing an existing Sub-processor; or

(c) materially changing the location or nature of the processing carried out by an existing Sub-processor.

5.2 You may object to a change on reasonable grounds relating to the protection of personal data, by email to privacy@ledgeraa.com within 14 days of the notice. Ledgeraa shall consider any objection in good faith. If the objection cannot be resolved, your rights are as set out in clause 4.2 of the DPA, including the refund described in clause 6.2(d) of the Terms.

5.3 If you do not object within 14 days of the notice, you are deemed to have accepted the change.

5.4 Where a replacement is required urgently for reasons of security or continuity of service, Ledgeraa shall give notice as soon as reasonably practicable, with the reasons for the urgency, in accordance with clause 4.3 of the DPA. Your right to object under clause 5.2 applies from the date of that notice.

5.5 Changes to Part B or Part C shall be reflected on this page and, where material, in our Privacy Policy.
