**Version 1.0 · Effective 1 November 2026**

This Data Processing Agreement (the "DPA") forms part of the agreement between you and Ledgeraa LTD ("Ledgeraa") under the [Ledgeraa Terms of Service](https://www.ledgeraa.com/terms) (the "Terms"). It applies where Ledgeraa processes Customer Personal Data on your behalf in providing the Service, and sets out the terms required by Article 28(3) of the UK GDPR. You accept this DPA by accepting the Terms, and no separate signature is required.

### 1. Definitions and interpretation

1.1 Capitalised terms not defined in this DPA have the meanings given in the Terms. In this DPA:

**"Aggregated Statistics"** has the meaning given in clause 3.9.

**"Commission"** means the Information Commission, or any successor supervisory authority in the United Kingdom.

**"Customer Personal Data"** means personal data relating to persons other than you that is contained in Customer Data. This includes the names and contact details of your clients and suppliers, and personal data appearing on invoices and receipts.

**"Data Protection Law"** means the UK GDPR, the Data Protection Act 2018, the Privacy and Electronic Communications (EC Directive) Regulations 2003, and any other law relating to the processing of personal data that applies in the United Kingdom. In each case this means that law as amended from time to time, including by the Data (Use and Access) Act 2025.

**"domestic law"** means the law of the United Kingdom or of a part of the United Kingdom.

**"Personal Data Breach"** means a breach of security leading to the accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorised disclosure of, or access to, Customer Personal Data transmitted, stored or otherwise processed by Ledgeraa or its Sub-processors.

**"Sub-processor"** means any third party engaged by Ledgeraa to process Customer Personal Data.

**"Sub-processors Page"** means the page published at ledgeraa.com/subprocessors.

**"UK GDPR"** has the meaning given in section 3(10) of the Data Protection Act 2018.

1.2 The terms "controller", "processor", "data subject", "personal data" and "processing" have the meanings given in the UK GDPR.

1.3 Clause 1.2 of the Terms applies to this DPA. References to clauses are to clauses of this DPA unless they are expressed to be clauses of the Terms.

### 2. Roles and scope

2.1 In respect of Customer Personal Data, you are the controller and Ledgeraa is your processor.

2.2 You shall ensure that:

(a) you have a lawful basis for the processing of Customer Personal Data;

(b) you have provided all information to data subjects that Data Protection Law requires;

(c) Customer Personal Data is accurate; and

(d) your instructions to Ledgeraa comply with Data Protection Law.

2.3 Ledgeraa's processing of personal data relating to you as a user of the Service is governed by the [Privacy Policy](https://www.ledgeraa.com/privacy), not by this DPA.

2.4 **Submissions and payments.**

(a) You instruct Ledgeraa to transmit to HMRC each Submission that you approve, together with the information it contains. HMRC receives that information as a controller in its own right and is not a Sub-processor. Ledgeraa is not responsible for HMRC's processing of it.

(b) Information that HMRC requires Ledgeraa to send with each request to HMRC, including fraud-prevention information, relates to you as a user of the Service. So does information processed to charge Fees, including through Stripe. That information is governed by the Privacy Policy, not by this DPA.

2.5 **Domestic processing.** Some of your processing of Customer Personal Data may fall outside the scope of the UK GDPR by virtue of Article 2(2)(a) of the UK GDPR.

(a) Ledgeraa shall nonetheless process that Customer Personal Data in accordance with clauses 3 to 5 as though you were its controller.

(b) Clause 2.2 applies to you only to the extent that Data Protection Law applies to you.

### 3. Ledgeraa's obligations

3.1 **Documented instructions.** Ledgeraa shall process Customer Personal Data only on your documented instructions, including with regard to transfers of Customer Personal Data to a third country or an international organisation, unless required to do so by domestic law to which Ledgeraa is subject.

(a) Your instructions consist of the Terms, this DPA, and your use and configuration of the Service.

(b) Where domestic law requires Ledgeraa to process Customer Personal Data otherwise than on your instructions, Ledgeraa shall inform you of that requirement before processing. This does not apply where that law prohibits such information on important grounds of public interest.

(c) Ledgeraa shall inform you immediately if, in its opinion, an instruction infringes Data Protection Law.

3.2 **Confidentiality.** Ledgeraa shall ensure that all persons it authorises to process Customer Personal Data have committed themselves to confidentiality, or are under an appropriate statutory obligation of confidentiality.

3.3 **Security.** Ledgeraa shall implement and maintain the technical and organisational measures described in Annex 2, as required by Article 32 of the UK GDPR. Ledgeraa may update those measures from time to time, provided that the overall level of protection is not reduced.

3.4 **Data subject requests.** The Service enables you to locate, correct, export and delete Customer Personal Data.

(a) Taking into account the nature of the processing, Ledgeraa shall assist you by appropriate technical and organisational measures, insofar as this is possible, in responding to requests from data subjects exercising their rights under Data Protection Law.

(b) If Ledgeraa receives such a request directly, it shall forward the request to you promptly, and in any event within 5 Business Days. It shall not respond to the request, except to direct the data subject to you or on your documented instructions.

(c) Ledgeraa may charge a reasonable fee, notified to you in advance, for assistance under clause 3.4(a) or clause 3.5 that goes materially beyond the functionality of the Service. This does not apply where the assistance is required because of Ledgeraa's breach of this DPA.

3.5 **Assistance with compliance.** Taking into account the nature of the processing and the information available to it, Ledgeraa shall provide reasonable assistance to you in ensuring compliance with your obligations under Articles 32 to 36 of the UK GDPR, in relation to:

(a) the security of processing;

(b) the notification of Personal Data Breaches to the Commission, and their communication to data subjects;

(c) data protection impact assessments; and

(d) prior consultation with the Commission.

3.6 **Personal Data Breaches.**

(a) Ledgeraa shall notify you without undue delay after becoming aware of a Personal Data Breach, and shall use reasonable endeavours to do so within 72 hours. It shall notify you by email to the address associated with your Account and, where appropriate, by notice within the Service.

(b) So far as the information is available to it, Ledgeraa shall describe:

- the nature of the Personal Data Breach;
- the categories and approximate number of data subjects and records concerned;
- its likely consequences; and
- the measures taken or proposed to address it.

Where that information cannot be provided at the same time, it may be provided in phases without undue further delay.

(c) Ledgeraa shall take reasonable steps to contain, investigate and mitigate the Personal Data Breach. It shall also provide reasonable assistance to you in notifying the Commission and communicating with data subjects under Articles 33 and 34 of the UK GDPR.

(d) An unsuccessful attempt or activity that does not compromise the security of Customer Personal Data is not a Personal Data Breach. Examples include pings, port scans and failed log-in attempts.

(e) Ledgeraa's notification of, or response to, a Personal Data Breach is not an acknowledgement by Ledgeraa of any fault or liability.

3.7 **Return and deletion.**

(a) The provision of the Service may end on deletion of your Account under clause 6.5 of the Terms, on termination under clause 16 of the Terms, or on discontinuation of the Service under clause 16.5 of the Terms. Before it ends, you may choose to have Customer Personal Data returned to you by exporting it using the Service. Where you are unable to do so, Ledgeraa shall provide Customer Personal Data to you in a commonly used, machine-readable format, if you request it in writing within the applicable export period, unless domestic law prohibits this.

(b) Ledgeraa shall then delete Customer Personal Data from its active systems within 7 days. Copies held in backups shall be deleted as those backups expire, and in any event within 14 days.

(c) Clause 3.7(b) does not apply to Customer Personal Data that domestic law requires Ledgeraa to retain. Ledgeraa shall retain that data only for so long as domestic law requires, shall keep it confidential, and shall not otherwise process it.

(d) On your written request, Ledgeraa shall confirm in writing that deletion under this clause 3.7 has been completed.

3.8 **Information and audit.**

(a) Ledgeraa shall make available to you, on request, all information necessary to demonstrate compliance with Article 28 of the UK GDPR and this DPA. This may include written responses to a reasonable security questionnaire, a summary of Ledgeraa's technical and organisational measures, and the certifications or audit reports of its Sub-processors.

(b) Where that information is not reasonably sufficient to demonstrate Ledgeraa's compliance, Ledgeraa shall allow for and contribute to audits, including inspections, conducted by you or by an independent auditor mandated by you, provided that:

(i) you give not less than 30 days' written notice, and the parties agree the scope, timing and duration of the audit in advance;

(ii) the audit is conducted remotely where reasonably practicable and otherwise between 9.00 and 17.00 on a Business Day, without unreasonable disruption to Ledgeraa's business;

(iii) any auditor is bound by written obligations of confidentiality and is not a competitor of Ledgeraa;

(iv) no more than one audit takes place in any 12-month period, unless required by the Commission or following a Personal Data Breach affecting Customer Personal Data;

(v) the audit does not extend to the systems of Sub-processors, in respect of which clause 3.8(a) applies; and

(vi) you bear the costs of the audit, including Ledgeraa's reasonable costs of time notified to you in advance, save where the audit reveals a material breach of this DPA by Ledgeraa.

3.9 **Ledgeraa's own purposes.**

(a) Ledgeraa may use Customer Personal Data to create statistics about use of the Service ("Aggregated Statistics"). Aggregated Statistics are aggregated and anonymised so that no individual is identified or identifiable, taking into account all means reasonably likely to be used. Ledgeraa shall use for that purpose only the minimum Customer Personal Data necessary. It shall not attempt to re-identify any individual from Aggregated Statistics, and shall not disclose Aggregated Statistics in a form that identifies you or your Business.

(b) Where Ledgeraa processes Customer Personal Data for the purposes described in clause 3.9(a), or in clause 6.5(e) or 11.2 of the Terms, it does so as a controller in its own right, in accordance with Data Protection Law and the Privacy Policy, and not on your behalf. Aggregated Statistics are not Customer Personal Data.

(c) Save as set out in this clause 3.9, Ledgeraa shall not process Customer Personal Data for its own purposes.

3.10 **Records and cooperation.**

(a) Ledgeraa shall maintain a record of all categories of processing activities carried out on your behalf, as required by Article 30(2) of the UK GDPR, and shall make it available to the Commission on request.

(b) Ledgeraa shall cooperate, on request, with the Commission in the performance of its tasks.

(c) Unless prohibited by law, Ledgeraa shall promptly notify you of any communication from the Commission that relates specifically to Customer Personal Data processed on your behalf.

3.11 **Restrictions.** Ledgeraa shall not:

(a) sell Customer Personal Data, or make it available to any third party for consideration;

(b) use Customer Personal Data for advertising or marketing;

(c) use Customer Personal Data to train artificial intelligence or machine-learning models, whether its own or a third party's; or

(d) combine Customer Personal Data with personal data obtained from other sources, except as necessary to provide the Service.

You may object within 14 days of that notice, and clause 4.2(b) applies.

4.4 **Sub-processor terms.** Ledgeraa shall engage each Sub-processor under a written contract that imposes data protection obligations providing no less protection for Customer Personal Data than those set out in this DPA. In particular, the contract shall provide sufficient guarantees that the Sub-processor will implement appropriate technical and organisational measures, so that the processing meets the requirements of Data Protection Law. Where a Sub-processor fails to fulfil its data protection obligations, Ledgeraa shall remain fully liable to you for the performance of that Sub-processor's obligations, subject to clause 6.

5.2 Ledgeraa shall ensure that each restricted transfer of Customer Personal Data complies with Chapter V of the UK GDPR. It shall rely on either of the following:

(a) regulations approving transfers to the relevant country or to a relevant class of recipient, including organisations certified under the UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework; or

(b) appropriate safeguards, including the International Data Transfer Agreement or the International Data Transfer Addendum, in respect of which Ledgeraa has reasonably and proportionately concluded that the data protection test is met.

5.3 If a transfer mechanism on which Ledgeraa relies ceases to be valid, Ledgeraa shall, without undue delay, either adopt an alternative mechanism that complies with Chapter V of the UK GDPR or cease the transfer. It shall notify you if neither is possible.

5.4 Unless prohibited by law, Ledgeraa shall notify you without undue delay if it receives a legally binding request from a public authority for disclosure of Customer Personal Data. It shall disclose only the minimum Customer Personal Data necessary to comply.

### Annex 2: Technical and organisational measures

- **Encryption.** Data is encrypted in transit using TLS and at rest. HMRC access tokens are additionally encrypted and are never sent to users' browsers.
- **Segregation.** Each user's data is segregated by database access controls (row-level security). Files are held in per-user storage locations and are made available only through time-limited links.
- **Access control.** Only personnel authorised by Ledgeraa may access production systems, on a least-privilege basis. Such personnel access Customer Personal Data only where necessary to provide the Service, to provide support you have requested, to maintain security or to comply with law. Multi-factor authentication is required for access to the systems that hold Customer Data.
- **Devices.** Devices used to access production systems use full-disk encryption, automatic screen locking and current security updates.
- **Authentication.** User passwords are stored only as secure hashes, and a password-complexity policy is enforced. Users are signed out automatically after 10 minutes of inactivity, and session tokens are deleted from the user's browser on sign-out.
- **Resilience and recovery.** Ledgeraa maintains backups sufficient to restore the availability of, and access to, Customer Personal Data in a timely manner. Restoration from backup is tested at least [every six months]. Automated daily backups are encrypted and retained for no longer than 7 days.
- **Testing.** Ledgeraa regularly assesses the effectiveness of these measures, including through automated dependency and vulnerability scanning and the prompt application of security patches.
- **Secure development.** Changes to the Service are version-controlled and reviewed before deployment to production.
- **Logging.** Access to production systems and administrative actions are logged. Logs are reviewed in response to alerts and periodically.
- **Incident response.** Ledgeraa maintains a documented incident-response procedure. It includes notifying affected customers in accordance with clause 3.6 and, where required, notifying HMRC under HMRC's terms of use for software developers.
- **Supplier management.** Sub-processors are selected with regard to their security standards and are bound in accordance with clause 4.4.
- **Vulnerability disclosure.** A responsible-disclosure procedure is published at [ledgeraa.com/security](https://www.ledgeraa.com/security).
